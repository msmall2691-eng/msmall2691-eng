- **Business systems:** requirements, process mapping, tool evaluation, phased rollouts
- **Building:** React, TypeScript, Python/FastAPI, PostgreSQL, REST APIs and webhooks
- **Data and reporting:** SQL, Power BI, pandas, KPI and revenue reporting
- Founder of **M Studio** (websites and business tools) and **The Maine Cleaning Company**
- **B.S. Business Analytics & AI**, Computer Science minor, University of Southern Maine (in progress)
