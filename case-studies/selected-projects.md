# Megan Small | Selected project case studies

Business operations, systems and AI-assisted development.

## BrightBase

Cleaning operations platform

https://github.com/msmall2691-eng/Bright-Space

### Business need

Coordinate incoming requests, quotes, jobs, crews and billing without losing information between steps.

### My contribution

Translate experience running a cleaning business into workflows, develop and iterate on the platform using AI-assisted development, and review behavior against operational needs.

### What the project includes

Request intake and duplicate handling; staff-reviewed quotes; quote acceptance and scheduling; native crew assignments; completion checklists and photos; draft invoices; recurring-job generation.

### Evidence and outcome

The current repository documents the full workflow and safeguards against duplicate intake, quote acceptance and invoice creation. Job completion creates a draft invoice for review. This is evidence of implemented workflows, not a measured claim about hours saved or production reliability.

### Technologies

Python / FastAPI, React / Vite, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Docker, Railway.

### Next measurement

Track request-to-quote time, scheduling corrections and minutes spent preparing invoices.

## M Studio

Websites and practical business tools

https://mlinx.studio/tools

### Business need

Give small businesses useful tools and a clear path from a website visit to an inquiry.

### My contribution

Build and iterate on M Studio’s website and browser tools, combining service-business knowledge, interface design and AI-assisted development.

### What the project includes

Instant quotes, invoice maker, email signatures, digital cards, QR codes, tracked links, website checks and a cost-of-manual-work calculator.

### Evidence and outcome

The live quote tool was exercised with changes to property size and an add-on. A four-bedroom, three-bathroom biweekly standard clean showed $189–$222; five bedrooms showed $204–$239; adding an oven showed $229–$268. These are demo estimates, not client charges.

### Technologies

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vercel.

### Next measurement

Track tool usage, completed inquiries and time needed to prepare a quote.
